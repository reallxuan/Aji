Settings, About, and the no-engine screen

The gear in the header swaps the panel body for settings, and the board stays where it is. Every change applies at once, with no Save button and no restart. The item marked new is my proposal and wasn't on your list.

S. Settings. Four groups. The disc lines are any two of win rate, score lead and visits, with a live preview disc. The analysis limit is empty by default, meaning no limit. The engine group names what is installed and holds G1's restart and stop.
