二 形势

4 归属怎么画

Move 80, Black to play. KataGo's ownership for every point, plus two stones it reads as dead: White F3 at the bottom and White R16 at the top right. A 方块: a square on each point, black or white for the side expected to own it, larger the surer KataGo is. On a dead stone the square is the other colour, on top of the stone. This is the common style: precise and readable point by point, but busy. B 色块: every point's cell is tinted darker (Black's) or lighter (White's), so areas read as regions, the way you'd shade a map. Dead stones are drawn faded. It's calm, but tints on wood look muddy where an area is uncertain. C 只标定型: like A, but only points KataGo is fairly sure of (over 60%), all one size. Contested areas stay bare wood. That's exactly what 形势判断 counts, so the board and the count agree. My call: C. The board stays quiet, and every square you see is one point in the count.

5 形势判断：开关与数字

One switch shows both: the board gets the squares, and the panel gets a line under the win-rate bar with the count. 显示 → 形势判断 in the macOS menu bar (视图 on Windows), and the key T. The candidate discs stay on while it's shown; ✕ or T turns it off. It follows the current position as you navigate. The count is area scoring under Chinese rules (or territory under Japanese rules, following the rules chip). Every point is given to the side KataGo leans to, so the two counts add up to 361 and the margin lands close to the bar's lead. The squares on the board show only the points that are settled.
