一 候选

1 排序与占比

Left, today: KataGo's order, six columns. Right, M2: 与最佳相比 is off by default (it stays in the column menu), and the move actually played gets a small 实战 tag beside its point instead. Next, you can click a column header to sort by it, and click again to reverse. The rank chips keep KataGo's numbers, so a chip still matches its disc on the board. Clicking the active header a third time goes back to KataGo's order. Share of visits goes inside the 计算量 cell instead of a new column: a hairline bar under the number, plus the percentage. You can see at a glance how sure KataGo is, without the table getting wider. My call: the sort is remembered per device, like the layout.

二 波动

3 波动列表

A new panel, 波动. In the default layout it's a tab beside 候选, and the layout menu can put it anywhere, like any panel. Each side gets its own list of moves that lost more than the threshold, biggest loss first. The controls set what counts as a loss: 按 win rate or score, the threshold, and 仅后续 (only moves after the current one). The loss is the same number everywhere: the mover's win rate (or score) before the move minus after it, from whole-game analysis. The candidate discs' tiers already use it. My call: clicking a row goes to the position before that move. You see the candidates KataGo preferred, with the move actually played ringed among them (the next-move hint, B). The row for the current move is highlighted.
