1 进入点目

Two ways in. The game menu has 终局点目. And when 形势判断 is on at the last move of a game that has ended (two passes, or a result in the record), its line offers 点目. Scoring mode hides the candidate discs and pauses analysis on the board, so the count stays still while you mark stones.

2 点目中（可以点）

KataGo marked White C5 and Black H4 as dead. The panel's top part (the bar and the graph) gives way to the count until you leave. Click a stone to flip its whole group. Try H4: once it is alive, White's area on the right is no longer White's alone, and the count drops sharply. When you change KataGo's marking, 已改动 shows next to the result, and 恢复 KataGo 的判断 puts it back. My call: neutral points (dame) get no square. Under Chinese rules they count for nobody, and the bare wood shows where they are.

3 日韩规则：数目

With the rules chip on 日韩, the same position counts territory: the points inside each side's area (a dead stone's point included), plus each dead stone again as a prisoner taken off at the end, plus stones captured during the game, plus komi. The table has those columns instead.
