A new black board like 印刷 (Printed): how black?

Three blacks, each drawn the way Printed is: no grain, thin lines, no shadow, flat stones. The cards sit on the dark theme's panel colour #1E1E21, and the page on the window colour #161618. The button at the top right switches to the light interface.

So that they read on black, the stones and marks are already adjusted as listed under "On the board" below. This page asks only about the base colour and the lines; the stones and the candidates come next.

A 石墨 (Graphite)

a step lighter than the panel
  • Base #2A2A2E
  • The board stands out by being a little lighter than its surroundings, with no shadow
  • Black stones are darker than the board, so contrast is best

B 墨黑 (Ink)

darker than the window
  • Base #0E0E10
  • The blackest, like a dark screen, and the least glaring at night
  • Black stones are nearly the board's colour and are told apart only by a light edge

C 暖黑 (Warm black)

a hint of wood
  • Base #25211C, lines slightly cream
  • Keeps the principle that the board is the one warm object on screen
  • Next to the neutral grey interface, it reads as another material

On the board (the same on all three, confirmed separately next)

  • Black stones: pure black with a thin light edge (today's flat black stone has a dark edge, which vanishes on black). White stones: a little darker than today, to glare less.
  • Candidates: paper discs and cinnabar rings as today; the ring of the move actually played turns from dark brown to light, or it cannot be seen on black.
  • The variation badge turns from a dark badge with a light letter to a light badge with a dark letter; marks and labels on empty points are light.
  • The last-move dot is unchanged: dark on a white stone, light on a black one.
