M6: opening, reading and stepping through game records

Each frame below is drawn from real data: the board is the derived GBK fixture of the 2025 Samsung Cup final, game 2 (Ding Hao vs Liao Yuanhe). The full-window frames are 1440 × 900 shown at 70 %; panel crops are at 100 %. The stones are a first pass at interface design 5.2 (slate black, shell white, contact and soft shadows); the app draws them the same way.
  • The title opens the game menu (new, open, recent, save as).
  • The chip beside it always shows the rules and komi the engine is using, and opens the game's information and analysis rules. It gets a small amber dot when you have changed them from what the file says.
  • Problems show as one line under the header; nothing pops up over the board.

C. Game information and analysis rules. Clicking the chip opens this. The top half is the record's information. The bottom half sets what KataGo analyses with, for this game only. Chinese rules mean area scoring at 7.5; Japanese/Korean rules mean territory scoring at 6.5; neither lets the komi change. Custom (自定义) frees the komi (any value in steps of 0.5) and lets you choose area (数子) or territory (数目) scoring. A change re-analyses at once. The line under the controls says what the file wrote and how Aji read it, with a link back to that reading. Save as writes your choice into the file's RU and KM.

D. Jumping to a move. Clicking the move number turns it into a field with the number selected; type and press Enter. Escape or clicking away cancels. ← → (and ↑ ↓) step, Home and End jump to the ends, and the wheel or trackpad over the board steps one move at a time.

Changing the default never changes a game already open. On macOS the same file commands also live in the system menu bar; Windows and Linux have no menu bar, as the interface design asks.
