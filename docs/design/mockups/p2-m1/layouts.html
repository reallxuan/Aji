P2 · M1 · 布局 layouts (decision P2)

The same position as the board styles page, Black to move at move 58. Windows are drawn at their real pixel size and scaled down to fit.
  • Rules every layout keeps (interface design section 1): the board is a square sized by the window height, nothing takes vertical space from it, and the panels are one surface divided by hairlines, never floating cards.
  • Presets (sections 1 to 5) follow LizzieYzy's modes as charter 7.9 lists them. Each is a starting point the user can then change by dragging (section 6).
  • Wide windows (section 7) settle the interface design's open item 4: extra width becomes a second panel column.
  • The phone layout (section 8) is its own layout for touch, not the desktop shrunk. Phase 2 builds and tests it in a narrow window and in a browser; Phase 3 serves it to phones.
  • Presets are chosen from a 布局 entry in the game menu and in settings, with 恢复默认 to go back.

1 · 默认 Default

Today's screen, now made of panels: the header row, the win-rate bar and graph, the candidate list, and one tab group holding the variation tree and comments. With no variations and no comment, the tab group folds away, as the variations section does today. The default for every window between 1200 and 1900 px wide.

2 · 经典 Classic

LizzieYzy's classic arrangement: the board in the middle, the graph and comments on the left, candidates and the tree on the right. Both columns are narrow at 1440 px (286 px each), so the header row moves to the right column's top and the rules chip folds into the title.

3 · 极简 Minimal

The board and a slim column: header row, win-rate bar and the top three candidates. The extra width becomes even margins around the board. For reviewing with as little on screen as possible, or a small laptop window.

4 · 思考 Thinking

A second large board (the sub-board) shows the best line from the current position, stepped by the wheel while the pointer is over it. The win-rate bar and a short candidate list sit under it. The main board stays clear of numbered stones, which is the point of this mode.

5 · 四盘 Four boards

Three sub-boards show the lines of the top three candidates, each labelled with its rank chip, win rate and lead. The fourth cell holds the bar and the graph. Clicking a sub-board's label plays that line on the main board as a hovered line.

6 · Rearranging: dragging a panel

Each panel shows a grip at its top left only while the pointer is over it, so the surface stays clean. Dragging the grip lifts the panel; dropping it on a panel's edge splits that place, and dropping it on a panel's middle adds a tab. Blue marks where it would land. Hairlines between panels drag to resize. On touch, a long press on the panel's grip lifts it. Panels can also be hidden from the 布局 menu; the board cannot be moved or hidden.

7 · Wide windows

At 1920 × 1080 the board is 1048 px and the panel 840 px. Above a panel width of about 760 px, the default layout splits into two columns: analysis on the left (bar, graph, candidates), the record on the right (tree, comments, sub-board). Below that it stays one column. Proposed answer to interface design 4's open item.

8 · 手机 Phone (375 × 812) and the narrow window

Below about 700 px of width, or when the window is taller than wide, the layout changes: the board spans the full width, the win-rate bar sits under it, and the panels become tabs below. Navigation moves to large buttons along the bottom edge, in thumb reach. The header keeps the title and the engine dot. Tapping a candidate shows its line (as hovering does), and a swipe across the board steps through it. A second tap on the same candidate plays it (from M3). Each tab is a full panel, so the panels built in M2 to M4 appear here with no extra work. Board style: printed and flat by default here (board styles page, section 3).
