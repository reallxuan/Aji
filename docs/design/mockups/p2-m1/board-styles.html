P2 · M1 · 棋盘与棋子样式 board and stone styles (decision P1)

Position: move 58 of the M6 sample game (2025 Samsung Cup final, game 2), Black to move, with the M7 candidates. Candidate data is made up. The page background switches between the dark and light themes (top right), because a board that looks right on one may not on the other.
  • What to decide: which boards and which stone sets ship in Phase 2. The plan asks for at least two sets in each theme. A "set" is one board plus one kind of stones, chosen together in settings, with an option to mix them.
  • The candidate discs (interface design 5.3) stay the same on every board: paper discs with cinnabar and brown rings. Section 4 checks they still read on each.
  • Not proposed: importing your own board or stone images (decision P12, left for Phase 8).

1 · Boards

Each with today's stones, so only the board changes. A is what Aji draws today. B is lighter and finer, like a kaya board. C is a dark walnut for night use with the dark theme; its lines and coordinates turn light. D is a flat printed surface, like a diagram in a Go book: no grain, thin lines, meant for the flat stones, screenshots and small phone screens.

2 · Stones

Close-up of the upper right, on board A. 1 · Slate and shell is today's realistic pair (interface design 5.2). 2 · Yunzi (云子): matte black with a faint green edge and no shine; white is warm, creamy and a little translucent, with no grain. 3 · Flat: solid fills with a thin outline and no shadow, as in printed diagrams.

3 · Proposed sets

The pairings settings would offer. Recommended to ship: all four. The default stays Honey · slate and shell in both themes, so nothing changes for anyone who does not look. Walnut is offered in both themes but suggested for dark; Printed is the default of the phone layout (M1's small-screen layout) because flat stones stay legible at 18 px per point.

4 · Candidates and the hovered line on each board

Left: candidate discs. Right: the best move's line, stepped to move 5. The numbers on the line's stones keep their colours on every board. On walnut, the discs' paper fill gives them more contrast than on honey; on the printed board the shadow under the best disc is dropped.
