The candidate discs hide while 标记 is on, so a mark is never covered; the table keeps updating. Outside markup mode, marks from the file are always drawn, and a disc on a marked point draws over it.

清除标记 removes every mark on this move, as one undoable step. Marks belong to a move: stepping away and back shows them again.

On touch the tools are the same row; freehand follows the finger, and a swipe does not navigate while ✎ is on.

3 摆子 setup

● adds black stones, ○ white, ◐ alternates. A click on a stone of the chosen colour removes it. Drag a stone to move it. Right-click a stone (long press on touch) to remove it or flip its colour. 轮到 sets the side to move (SGF PL). 停一手 passes. 插入一手 puts a move before the next recorded one and keeps the rest of the line after it. 折叠为新局面 turns this position into the start of a new record (LizzieYzy's 折叠已落棋子), and can be undone.

Where setup stones go. At a move of the record, the first setup edit starts a new variation with a setup node, so the record's later moves are never changed under you. At a node that is already setup only (the root of a problem, say), edits change that node in place.

已修改 in the header marks unsaved changes, in every mode. It clears on save, or when undo returns to the saved state.

The dashed ghost on R8 is the white stone from R9, being dragged. Q10 and P10 are black stones just added. The menu is a right-click on the white stone on C6.

4 评论 comments

A panel, 评论. It shows the comment of the move on the board (SGF C), and is edited in place: click into it and type. The text is kept when the field loses focus or you move to another position, as one undoable step per visit. Moves with a comment get a dot in the 分支 tree. In the wide default layout it sits in the record column under the tree; elsewhere it is a tab beside 候选 and 波动.
