Position: move 58 of the M6 sample game (2025 Samsung Cup final, game 2), Black to move. Candidate and ownership data are made up.
  • 1. A 形势判断 switch in the edit row, next to 试下, 标记 and 摆子.
  • 2. A square on the point under the mouse, in the colour of the side to move, like Fox (野狐). Not a ghost stone.

  • Boards in sections 2 and 3 react to the mouse. The page background switches between dark and light (top right).

1. 形势判断 in the edit row

Today 形势判断 is reached only by T and the 显示 (视图) menu. The new button is the same switch: pressing it, pressing T or the ✕ on the line all turn the same thing on and off. It is disabled during 终局点目, like the other three. Hover shows 形势判断 (T).
A puts it straight after 摆子. B puts a thin divider before it, because it is a view, not a board mode: 试下, 标记 and 摆子 exclude each other, while 形势判断 can stay on during any of them. Recommended: B.

2. The point under the mouse

A filled square on the empty point under the mouse, in the colour of the side to move. Move the mouse over the boards. It shows only where a click would place a stone: not on stones, not on candidate discs (they have their own hover, the line), not in 标记 mode, not during 终局点目, and never on touch.
One thing to settle: 形势判断 already draws squares on the board, at 36% of the spacing. A hover square of the same size would read as one more point of territory. A uses that size anyway. B is larger (52%) with a contrasting rim, so it stays distinct with the estimate on. Recommended: B.

3. Depth for the wood boards

Superseded. The owner's goal was material quality, which thickness did not give. A throwaway page compared photographed grain instead, and the design (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-p2-ui-polish-design.md, section 3) follows it. This section is kept as the record of what was tried.

Four ingredients, each independent. Shadow: the board casts a soft shadow onto the window, so it sits above it. Light: a faint light from the upper left across the wood, and a bevelled rim (light top and left edge, dark bottom and right). Thickness: the board's front edge shows as a darker band of end grain; it costs the grid about 2.5% of its size. Inlaid lines: each grid line gets a faint light line below and right of it, as if cut into the wood. The stones already have shadows (slate and yunzi) and are unchanged.
Recommended: shadow + light as the default for every wood board. They change no geometry and cost nothing per frame, since the board is drawn once. Thickness and inlaid lines are shown so you can judge them.
